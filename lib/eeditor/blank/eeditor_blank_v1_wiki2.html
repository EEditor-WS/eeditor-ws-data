Overview Brief · Declassified · Category I

A world on the brink. Fifteen years have passed since the end of the Great War, yet peace remains elusive. Radical regimes have risen in the heart of Europe, a communist giant solidifies in the east, and old empires cling desperately to their colonies.

In 1936, the world enters a new phase of global conflict. The German Reich builds the VECTOR alliance, the USSR forms the COMINTERN, and the Japanese Empire ignites war in Asia. The fate of civilisation hangs by a thread.

This is the archive of the Second Alternative World War — its events, its powers, and its operations. Every dossier is declassified. Every operation documented. Read at your own risk.

Declassified general staff plans

Operation "Cut"

EXECUTOR: GERMANY + SWEDEN

Joint invasion of Norway and Denmark. Division of northern spheres: Germany takes Denmark, Sweden takes Norway.

EXECUTOR: SWEDEN

Northern offensive. Goal — creation of a unified Scandinavian bloc under Swedish protectorate.

Operation "Beach"

EXECUTOR: JAPANESE EMPIRE

Seizure of Malaysia to dominate the Pacific. Utilises Japanese naval superiority.

Operation "Trees"

EXECUTOR: JAPANESE EMPIRE

Military operation in Vietnam after France's withdrawal. Supported by Germany.

Operation "Pacific"

EXECUTOR: AMERICAN CONFEDERACY

Operation "Sauce"

EXECUTOR: ITALY

Operation "Desert"

Military operation against Morocco over trade disputes. Morocco refuses friendship — Spain acts.

Operation "Taiga"

EXECUTOR: USSR

Offensive against Finland. The time has come. Finnish forest ambush tactics — the main threat.

Operation "Overblu"

EXECUTOR: EMPIRE OF NORTHERN ALESKAN

Strike by loyal imperial divisions against the Blue Column rebels in the south. Breaking through defences.

Northern offensive, rapid breakthrough through defence lines, capture of the capital. War begins 3 months after activation.

Plan "Renyatom"

Classified nuclear weapons project. Goal — strengthen the state and continue the elimination of "inferior" peoples.

Ideologies

Political doctrines of the era

Communism

Dictatorship of the proletariat, planned economy, international revolution. Leader of the global movement — the USSR. The Comintern unites France, Great Britain, and the Union into a single red bloc.

Fascism

Radical nationalism, totalitarianism, cult of the leader. The Reich, Italy, Japan. Vector — a military alliance of fascist powers aimed at redrawing the world.

Monarchy

Traditional dynastic rule. Empire of Northern Aleskan (Kirill VII), Order of Salvation of Bavaria (Holy Regent), Japanese Empire. Many regimes balance between absolutism and constitutionalism.

Theocracy

Power from God. Strengthening the role of religion in the state: Catholicism, Orthodoxy, Protestantism, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism — each faith builds its own "sacred" state.

Democracy

Popular sovereignty, parliament, elections. Old democracies (Britain, France, USA) under pressure from totalitarian regimes. Social democracy as a compromise.

Trade Republic

Rule by merchants and corporations. Free trade as the foundation of the state. Gallic SDR, some city-states. Profit above ideology.

Oligarchy

Concentration of power in the hands of the wealthy. Brazilian junta, some Latin American regimes. Capital rules.

Neutrality

Non-intervention policy. Switzerland, some minor powers. "We are outside the game." But how long will this peace last?

Alliances & Blocs

Military-political coalitions of the era

VECTOR

FOUNDER: GERMAN REICH

"New World Order". A military alliance created by Germany to reshape the world. Members: Italy, Turkey, Bulgaria, Hungary, Romania (attempted), Japan, Iran (attempted), Confederates (attempted), Russian State (attempted). Goal — destruction of the Comintern and old democracies.

COMINTERN

FOUNDER: USSR

"The Great and Mighty Alliance". Unites communist countries: France (after revolution), Great Britain (after protests), USSR. Goal — saving the world from capitalism. A worthy opponent of Vector.

ALLIES (ANTANTÉ)

TRADITIONAL BLOC

Great Britain, France (until its fall), their dominions and colonies. After France fell, the bloc effectively dissolved, but resistance continues.

ANTI-SOVIET ALLIANCE

POLAND + GERMANY (proposed)

Germany's proposal to Poland for joint military action against the USSR. Alternative history: Poland may accept or decline.

BERLIN–MOSCOW

REICH–USSR PACT

"Now we have no equals. Victory is unavoidable." A temporary alliance of two totalitarian giants before inevitable betrayal.

SCANDINAVIAN UNION

SWEDEN + FINLAND

Unification of the northern peoples. Sweden proposes an alliance, Finland accepts. Goal — peace and enlightenment across all of Scandinavia.
